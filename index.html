Look up a creator

Type the handle without @, or paste their profile link. Add the fee they quoted to see cost per view and a verdict. The creator is saved to the shortlist.

Before you book a creator

Estimates what a new creator would deliver, based on how creators of the same tier in your shortlist perform. Useful for YouTube, Moj or any creator you can't look up.

Upload a creator sheet

Any Excel or CSV file works: a full sheet, a half-filled one, or just a list of creators. Every Instagram handle or profile link is looked up, and only the empty cells are filled. Whatever is already in your file stays as it is.

Fetching…

Instagram only finds creators by their exact handle (like itss.vanshikaaa) or profile link, not by display name. Rows with only a display name are added so you can fill them in.

Shortlist

How the numbers are worked out

          

            
Engagement
likes + comments

            
ER (followers)
avg engagements ÷ followers × 100

            
ER (views)
engagements ÷ views × 100

            
CPV
fee ÷ views

            
CPE
fee ÷ engagements

            
CPM
fee ÷ views × 1,000

            
CPR
fee ÷ average reach

            
Avg CPV / CPE / CPR
average of each creator’s value

            
Blended CPV / CPR
total fees ÷ total views (or reach)

            
Cost per 1K followers
fee ÷ followers × 1,000

            
ER (reach)
engagements ÷ reach × 100

            
Reach rate
reach ÷ followers × 100

            
View rate
avg views ÷ followers × 100

            
Budget used
spend ÷ budget × 100

            
Audience quality
score out of 100 from organic posts: ER for the size, comments vs likes, like spikes, Reel views vs followers, follow count

            
Sponsored vs organic
(sponsored ER − organic ER) ÷ organic ER × 100, boosted posts left out

            
Likely boosted
sponsored post with about 3× the creator’s normal views

          

          
Average Reach is not public on Instagram. When the team hasn’t entered it from the creator’s insights, it is estimated as 80% of average views (or a typical reach rate for the tier), so CPR is an estimate too.
