My Creator Desk privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

My Creator Desk is a web tool for brands and agencies that run influencer marketing. It helps teams look up public Instagram creator data and track campaigns.

What we use from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to identify you and open your own My Creator Desk spreadsheet. We do not read your Gmail, Drive files or any other Google data.

Where your data is stored

  • On your first sign-in, a private Google Sheet is created for you and shared with your email.
  • Creators, fees and campaign posts you add are saved in that sheet.
  • Other team members cannot see your sheet. The team admin can see the list of people who signed in and their sheets.

Instagram data

Creator numbers come from Instagram's public Business Discovery API: follower counts, likes, comments, views and captions of public business and creator accounts.

Deleting your data

You can remove creators at any time with the Remove and Remove all buttons, or delete rows in your sheet. To have your sheet and sign-in record removed, contact the admin at vanshikajuyal06@gmail.com.

If you connected an Instagram or Facebook account, you can disconnect it at any time from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → remove My Creator Desk. All data fetched with that connection is then deleted from My Creator Desk within 30 days.
