My Creator Desk (“we”, “us”) is an online dashboard for brands and agencies that run influencer marketing. It is operated by CREATOR DESK, a sole proprietorship registered under Udyam (UDYAM-DL-10-0134110), RZ-52A, Dharampura Phase-1, Najafgarh, New Delhi 110043, India. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It follows India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture. We do not read your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Company details you enter: company name, legal name, GSTIN and phone number.
  • Your work in the dashboard: creators you look up or add, fees, notes, campaign posts and campaign settings.
  • Instagram connection (company admins only): when an admin connects the company’s own Instagram professional account through Facebook Login, we store the account name, the linked Facebook Page name and an access token. The token is encrypted before it is stored and is never shown to anyone.
  • Public creator data: follower counts, posts, likes, comments, views and captions of public Instagram Business and Creator accounts, read through Meta’s official Instagram Graph API.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes all payments. We receive the order and payment reference, amount, plan and status. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank login details.
  • Usage records: which member made which lookup and when, so company admins can see usage and we can keep within Instagram’s limits.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you only the company workspaces you belong to.
  • To run lookups, calculate metrics (engagement rate, CPV, CPE, audience quality and similar) and save your work.
  • To process subscriptions, approve companies and prevent misuse.
  • To contact you about your account, billing or support requests.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Each company’s data is kept separate. People in one company can never see another company’s data.
  • Members see their own work. Company admins can see the work and usage of everyone in their company.
  • Our team can see company names, plans, payments and usage totals to run the service, but does not open your creators, posts or campaigns.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and company data: while your company uses My Creator Desk, and up to 90 days after it closes so you can come back or export it.
  • Cached Instagram answers: about 6 hours, then they are refreshed or removed.
  • Payment records: as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can edit or remove creators and posts at any time in the dashboard.
  • A company admin can disconnect Instagram at any time. The stored token is deleted straight away.
  • You can also remove My Creator Desk from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations. Data fetched with that connection is then deleted within 30 days.
  • To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account or your company’s data, email udayruhil.82@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

Security

Access is protected by Google sign-in. The database itself enforces which company and which person can read each row. Instagram tokens are encrypted, and payments are verified with Razorpay’s signatures.
